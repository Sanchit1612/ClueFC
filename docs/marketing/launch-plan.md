# Seven-day organic launch

Goal: get five real groups through a full match, learn what causes drop-off, then
work toward 25 completed games. These are working targets, not a forecast of reach
or a promise of users. Ad spend remains zero.

| Day | Action | Time | Decision signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask organisers of two existing football groups for a small playtest; host one match if accepted | 45 min | Can new players finish without coaching? |
| 2 | Publish the owner-approved r/WebGames post if rules/duplicate check pass | 30 min plus replies | Opt-in Reddit sessions and actual game starts; clean links may not identify the exact subreddit |
| 3 | Reply to useful feedback and fix the largest first-match problem | 30–60 min | Completion among decided matches; qualitative confusion |
| 4 | Send the approved r/playmygame moderator request; post only if appropriate and allowed | 20 min | Permission and useful feedback, not raw impressions |
| 5 | Host a second community game in a designated channel | 45 min | Groups requesting a rematch; record observations without inventing retention data |
| 6 | Share one real example of a clever clue on an owned profile or approved channel | 20 min | New match starts and scorecard shares, without exposing a live key |
| 7 | Review the dashboard, campaign links and feedback notes | 30 min | Continue the source that produces finished games; pause distribution if people cannot finish |

For each post, stay available to answer questions. Avoid copying identical text
across communities or turning ordinary football discussions into advertisements.

## Expansion rule
After at least 20 decided matches, use 60% completion as an initial internal
threshold to discuss expanding distribution. It is a chosen test threshold, not
an industry benchmark. If completion is lower, inspect errors, expired rooms and
first-play confusion before buying traffic. Keep solo, friends and Quick Match
separate; they solve different acquisition problems.

Seven-day return data require mature cohorts. First-week visitors will largely
be pending until day eight or later. Rematches can be reviewed sooner, but are
not the same as returning on another day.

## Honest measurement
- Human participations are non-bot seats per match, not unique acquired people.
- Visitors are consenting browser IDs; non-consenters, resets and multiple devices
  prevent a verified whole-audience user count.
- r/WebGames gets a clean link because of its referral-link rule. Referrers can
  still identify Reddit, but exact post attribution is not guaranteed.
- Do not report bots, QA matches, simulated soak traffic or targets as users.
- Data collection starts with this release; the dashboard cannot recover old
  impressions or traffic that was never recorded.

## Feedback note
After each observed group: date, source, number of players, game mode, finished
or dropped, confusing moment, requested another match (yes/no), next fix. Avoid
collecting real names, emails, secret room tokens or unrevealed board keys.
