# Human playtest checklist

1. Four independent browser profiles or devices join via room link. Claim one Spymaster on each team and start.
2. Give a clue, guess own/opponent/bench words; observe score and turn changes on every device.
3. Test Yellow Cards with one and two opponent words left. Test Red Card results.
4. Disconnect the active Spymaster for 30 seconds, then reconnect. Preserve role and draft.
5. Open the same room in a second tab. First tab shows takeover; second restores seat.
6. Background a phone for a minute, switch Wi-Fi/data, refresh, and rejoin.
7. Finish and rematch: roles rotate, sides swap, and no old words recur for three boards.
8. Save and share result image. Confirm no hidden key in guesser network responses before full time.
9. Test five real groups, including a casual fan; measure invite-to-clue, match duration and rematch rate.
10. Before public launch: configure Turnstile, extend moderation, add owner contact and monitoring, perform real concurrent load test, review product name/word list/policies.
