# CLUE FC product marketing context

Document version: v1
Last updated: 2026-11-11

## Product overview
CLUE FC is a free browser football word game. A Spymaster gives one word and a
number; teammates connect football names and terms on a 25-card board. Yellow
penalties and a red-card instant loss add football rules. Solo players can use
labelled bots; groups use private room codes. Public Quick Match also exists.
The current public address is https://cluefc.pages.dev. It is an open beta, not a
paid service. No game account is required. Age requirement: 13+.

## Audience and job
English-speaking football fans who enjoy word associations and friendly arguments.
Primary use: a short shared game with a football group, or a solo trial before
inviting friends. Initial audience/channel preference from the owner: Reddit and
football communities. Region is not restricted; founder is in India.

## Differentiation and objections
Football-specific connections, solo bot access, invitation codes, referee-card
penalties, a full-time MVP/statistics card and JPG export. These are implemented
features, not claims of uniqueness against every competitor.
- No friends online: start with bots. They use curated associations and sometimes
  misread clues; they do not understand arbitrary language like a chat model.
- Is it trivia? It is an association game; several words can share a clue.
- Is it official Codenames? No. Independent football game, not affiliated with
  Codenames, clubs, players or leagues. Do not imply endorsement or licensed art.
- Is it AI built? AI coding tools were used. Answer honestly; do not invent a
  hand-coded origin story or claim the game took months to build.

## Positioning and voice
Lead with a playable clue and what a group does together. Existing brand line:
“One clue. Your whole team.” Use plain football language: mates, clue, squad,
red card, room, full time. Avoid hype, fabricated social proof, bot-driven player
counts, guaranteed reach, engagement bait and mass identical cross-posts.

## Evidence
The working game, source repository and automated/live checks establish that it
is playable; tests and simulations are not users or testimonials. No verified
customer quotes, acquisition totals or retention benchmarks are available yet.
Legacy counters include QA and bots. New campaign and visitor data begin after
instrumentation and include only consenting browsers.

## Goals and operating constraints
First goal: recruit five small groups for real full-match feedback. Then target
25 completed games and identify which source produces repeat play. These are
planning targets, not forecasts or guarantees. The owner wants a useful private
analytics dashboard immediately. Track games by mode, opt-in first-touch sources,
source-to-play conversion, rematches, and seven-day-mature return cohorts.
Organic distribution first; no ad budget authorized. No posting, modmail or DMs
are authorized yet. Drafts and a calendar are approved task scope. Public posting
needs the owner's explicit approval of the content and destination.

## Community constraints
r/football explicitly prohibits app promotion; exclude it. r/WebGames requires a
name-first title, a direct browser-game URL, no required signup, and no referral
links; use the clean public URL. Its AI-content discussion means quality and
transparent maker disclosure matter. r/playmygame has free-play, description,
maker/flair and monthly frequency requirements, plus a low-effort/reskin rule;
seek moderator clarity before posting this Codenames-style football variant.

## Learnings
No validated channel or copy experiments yet. Do not promote a one-off anecdote
to a proven marketing claim.
